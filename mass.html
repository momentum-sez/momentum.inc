Flagship Product

Mass

Mass creates a company and then operates it end-to-end — formation, identity, compliance, treasury, governance, audit — with AI agents doing the regulated work, across many jurisdictions at once. Incorporation is the easy part everyone sells; keeping the entity lawful, current, and provable for years is the work Mass automates. Entities, ownership, financial instruments, identity, and consent are agent-drivable APIs serving human founders and autonomous agents alike, across 13 live jurisdictions and 23 compliance domains.

Core Primitive

The multi-harboured entity

A multi-harboured entity is a portfolio of jurisdictional contingent claims. One legal person simultaneously in several jurisdictions: incorporated in Delaware, banking through ADGM, fund administration in Cayman, regulatory registration in Singapore — operating as a single entity, governed by the composition of every jurisdiction it touches.

Compliance rules from different jurisdictions are combined point-by-point; the strictest wins. If Delaware permits and Singapore prohibits, the composed constraint is prohibit. Every operation the entity executes — a transfer, a filing, an issuance — checks against the strictest applicable rule across all 23 compliance domains and every jurisdiction the entity harbours in. The research note How Compliance Composes states the scope precisely.

The composed rule is also a pricing input. The set of jurisdictions that can hold a given claim — and the cost of holding it — follows directly from how those rules compose. Adding a jurisdiction takes an API call and a corridor. Leaving one takes a filing. Human teams do this work over months; agents on Mass do it continuously.

The same capability that runs a whole jurisdiction is callable from any other product. A company that needs to create a compliant entity, keep it compliant, or move value across a border embeds Mass, and the most frictionful part of its own product simply happens. Stripe turned accepting a payment — once a bank relationship, a merchant account, and months of integration — into a few lines of code. Mass turns creating and operating a compliant, cross-border company into a few lines of code.

Division of Labour

Mass issues. Moxie clears.

Every tradeable instrument is a claim — a future cash flow with a probability distribution over timing and amount. Equity, debt, sukuk, parametric insurance, options, futures, swaps, structured notes, royalties, real-world-asset instruments, parimutuel pools, prediction-market positions, redeemable loyalty entitlements: each is a claim. Derivatives are claims constructed from claims.

Mass is the substrate that issues and governs them. Entities form on Mass; ownership records and instruments live on Mass; identity attestations and multi-party consent flow through Mass; the 23-domain compliance rules that decide whether an action proceeds are computed on Mass. Moxie is the clearing-and-settlement layer for those claims and their derivatives. Moxie reads the entity envelope into every obligation transition, prices the claim, settles it across corridors, and binds reliance to the result.

Mass — Issues & Governs

Entity formation. Lex programs encoding claim terms. The composed 23-domain compliance rules. Multi-jurisdictional harbouring. Corridor terms between zones. Regulator-participated amendment.

Moxie — Clears & Settles

Pricing. Matching. Settlement. Risk reservation. Evidence and audit. Reliance binding for each cleared claim. Cross-corridor netting. Counterparty intelligence.

The two surfaces fit together along the claims axis: Mass issues; Moxie clears. Composition follows the corridor and kernel grammar. Each new zone deployment adds a node where entities form, claims are encoded, and corridors connect the local market to the rest of the network.

Multi-Jurisdiction Assets

Every asset carries its own compliance across borders.

Mass gives each institutional asset its own state machine and survivability across jurisdictions. An asset carries its compliance context and recomputes at each jurisdictional boundary. Three jurisdictions in 8 seconds. The research behind this is published, beginning with the essay One Entity in Many Jurisdictions.

Architecture

A kernel each government runs.

Mass is a rule engine and a runtime. The rule layer compiles each jurisdiction’s law into machine-checkable lawpacks; the runtime executes the regulated work against them, continuously, at machine speed. Every deployment is sovereign — a Rust service each government runs on its own infrastructure, its authoritative state written only by its own kernel. Evidence uses the digital-signature standards banks and governments already rely on; correctness is established by formal verification in CI. No shared ledger, no network to join.
