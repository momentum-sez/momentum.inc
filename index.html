The Thesis

The bridge is made of institutions

AI can analyze every jurisdiction on earth in seconds. It cannot incorporate a company in any of them. The institutions that govern economic life were designed for human participants in walled-off jurisdictions. That architecture is obsolete.

Momentum builds the programmable legal entities, organizations, and institutions that replace it. Every deployment sharpens the institutional world model. Every entity formed, every corridor crossed, every compliance evaluation adds to what the system knows about how 210 jurisdictions actually work.

For governments operating digital public infrastructure: Mass turns compliance routing, entity verification, and authority resolution into programmable functions. One API connects citizen identity, company registries, tax systems, and payment rails. Then it opens corridors to every other jurisdiction on the network.

A company incorporates under ADGM law, custodies assets through Hong Kong, holds a Delaware entity with Cayman fund administration, and moves between jurisdictions at machine speed. Momentum builds the institutional substrate that makes this movement programmable.
