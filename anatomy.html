What you are seeing

                    
When a developer calls POST /v1/operations/execute, Mass decomposes the operation into a directed acyclic graph of steps. Each step targets a specific Mass service, triggers compliance evaluation across relevant domains, and produces auditable artifacts — Verifiable Credentials, attestation records, and passport updates.

                    
The step DAG is jurisdiction-aware. Delaware incorporation requires 10 steps across 4 waves. ADGM requires 11 steps with a mandatory special resolution before name reservation. Seychelles IBC requires 13 steps including registered agent appointment, TIN registration with SRC, and IBC tax exemption evaluation under the IBC Act 2016.

                    
Every step that writes data passes through the compliance tensor — 23 domains, fail-closed. The tensor evaluates evidence (KYC attestations, sanctions screening, tax status) and blocks the operation if any mandatory domain returns NonCompliant. Steps within the same wave execute in parallel. Mass manages the dependency graph.
