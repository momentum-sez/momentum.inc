What Mass Is

The core functions a government needs, as APIs

Mass handles five things: forming companies, tracking ownership, collecting revenue, verifying identities, and recording agreements. These are the Entities, Ownership, Fiscal, Identity, and Consent primitives.

Each is exposed as an API. A company incorporation is an API call. So is a compliance filing, a tax payment, an ownership transfer. Each produces structured, timestamped, verifiable data.

The government owns and operates the system. Momentum builds and maintains it. All statutory fees stay with the government.

Primitives

Five primitives, one surface

01

Entities

Programmatic company formation. A Delaware LLC compliant across 23 regulatory domains, with banking rails provisioned, in under 48 hours.

02

Ownership

Tamper-evident cap tables with transfer restrictions, lockups, and rights of first refusal enforced programmatically. Cryptographic provenance on every transfer.

03

Fiscal

Entity-level accounts, settlement, and automated tax event reporting. Revenue collection happens as transactions occur, not months later in a filing.

04

Identity

Progressive verification scaled to action sensitivity. Accredited investor status verified without revealing net worth. Zero-knowledge proofs as the default.

05

Consent

Multi-party cryptographic authorization. Board resolutions, shareholder votes, and cross-border regulatory approvals enforced before execution.

GovOS

Real-time visibility for every agency

An operational console giving each agency real-time visibility into the activity it governs. The financial regulator sees compliance status across all regulated entities. The revenue authority sees collection as it happens. The company registry sees formation, amendment, and dissolution in real time.

Agencies that currently pass information on paper or in quarterly reports share a common data layer. Each sees only what its mandate requires. The data is structured, auditable, and continuous.
